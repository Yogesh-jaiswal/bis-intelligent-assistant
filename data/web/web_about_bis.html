About Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the National Standards Body of India established under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016. BIS is responsible for the harmonious development of activities of standardization, conformity assessment, quality assurance, marking, and product certification in India.

Core Objectives and Mandate

The primary mandate of BIS is to provide safe, reliable, quality goods, minimizing health hazards to consumers, promoting exports and imports substitute, and ensuring conformity assessment through standard formulation, product certification, hallmarking, and laboratory testing.

Key Functions of BIS

1. Standardization: Formulating Indian Standards (IS) across diverse engineering, chemical, electrical, food, and consumer product sectors.

2. Conformity Assessment: Operating Product Certification Schemes including the ISI Mark Scheme (Scheme-I) and Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS).

3. Hallmarking: Regulating gold and silver jewelry hallmarking for consumer protection.

4. Laboratory Testing: Operating a network of central and regional laboratories and recognizing private testing laboratories across India.

5. Foreign Manufacturers Certification: Granting licences to foreign manufacturers under the FMCS scheme to use the Standard Mark.
