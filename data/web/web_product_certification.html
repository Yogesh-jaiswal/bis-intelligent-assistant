BIS Product Certification Scheme (Scheme-I / ISI Mark)

The BIS Product Certification Scheme (Scheme-I) enables domestic and international manufacturers to use the Standard Mark (ISI Mark) on products conforming to relevant Indian Standards.

Mandatory Certification & Quality Control Orders (QCO)

While product certification is generally voluntary, the Government of India issues Quality Control Orders (QCOs) making BIS certification mandatory for certain products in public interest, human safety, and environmental protection. For example, electrical cables, wires, and conductors under IS 694 and IS 1554 are subject to mandatory certification.

Grant of Licence Process

The grant of licence involves submitting an application, factory inspection by BIS officers to verify manufacturing and testing infrastructure, drawing of samples for testing in BIS or BIS-recognized laboratories, and verification of the Quality Control Plan.
